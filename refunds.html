General rule: because Dinorun is a digital product with machine-bound activation, sales are generally final and non-refundable after the installer, license or activation code has been delivered or activated.

1. Before purchasing

Please review the product page, system requirements, supported profiles, experimental DINOHB status, single-machine license restriction and ROM requirements before purchasing. Ask questions at 38784757@qq.com or leehonxuan@gmail.com before placing an order.

2. Non-refundable situations

Except where an exception below or mandatory law applies, refunds are not provided for:

  • change of mind after digital delivery or activation;
  • failure to obtain legally acquired compatible game data;
  • attempts to use one license on another computer;
  • hardware, driver, antivirus or operating-system configurations outside the stated requirements;
  • limitations already disclosed on the product page, including the experimental status of DINOHB;
  • failure to read the product description, terms or activation instructions.

3. Situations we will review

Contact us promptly if there is a duplicate charge, the purchased product or activation was not delivered, the delivered license is invalid for the correctly submitted machine request, or a material reproducible defect prevents the primary supported profile from operating and we cannot provide a reasonable remedy.

A request does not guarantee a refund. We may first offer corrected delivery, activation assistance, troubleshooting or another reasonable remedy.

4. How to request review

Email 38784757@qq.com or leehonxuan@gmail.com with the order number, purchase email, machine request code if relevant, problem description and non-sensitive evidence. Requests should normally be made within seven calendar days of purchase or discovery of the delivery problem. This time period does not limit rights that cannot legally be waived.

5. Payment provider and mandatory rights

When a purchase is processed by PayPal or another provider shown at checkout, that provider may handle payment disputes, refunds and chargebacks under its own terms. Dinorun may review delivery and activation issues, but mandatory consumer rights in the buyer's jurisdiction remain unaffected.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact support before filing a payment dispute so we can investigate delivery or activation. Fraudulent or abusive chargebacks may result in suspension or termination of the associated license. This does not restrict a customer's lawful right to dispute an unauthorized or invalid charge.
