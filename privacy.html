隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 8 月 24 日

This English version and the Simplified Chinese version describe the same policy. If a translation differs, the English version controls to the extent permitted by law.

1. Who operates Dinorun

Dinorun is operated by an independent software developer based in mainland China. Questions about this policy or personal information can be sent to leehonxuan@gmail.com.

2. Information we may receive

  • Order information: name, email address, country, product, order number and payment status supplied by the payment provider shown at checkout. We do not receive your full card number.
  • Activation information: the machine request code, machine fingerprint information contained in that request, license ID and activation status needed to issue and support a single-machine license.
  • Support information: messages, diagnostic details and files that you voluntarily send when requesting help.
  • Website delivery data: GitHub Pages may process ordinary server logs such as IP address, browser type and request time to deliver and secure this website.

3. How information is used

We use information only to process and document orders, issue and verify licenses, deliver support, prevent abuse, respond to disputes, maintain security and meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

4. Local application data

Dinorun stores settings, license data and save states locally on your Windows computer. These files are not automatically uploaded to us. They are shared only if you choose to send them for support.

5. Service providers

Information may be processed by providers necessary to operate the product, including the payment provider shown at checkout, GitHub Pages as website host and the email provider used for customer support. Their own privacy terms also apply to their services.

6. Retention

Order, license and essential support records may be retained for up to seven years when needed for licensing, fraud prevention, tax, accounting, audit or dispute obligations. Other support material is deleted when it is no longer reasonably needed.

7. Security and international processing

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect records. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. No internet or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information, subject to identity verification and records we must retain by law or for legitimate licensing and transaction purposes. Contact the support email above.

9. Children

Dinorun is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes

We may update this policy when the product, providers or legal requirements change. The effective date on this page will be revised.

1. Dinorun 的运营者

Dinorun 由位于中国大陆的独立软件开发者运营。如对本政策或个人信息有疑问，请联系 leehonxuan@gmail.com。

2. 我们可能收到的信息

  • 订单信息：由记录商户服务商提供的姓名、邮箱、国家或地区、产品、订单号和付款状态。我们不会收到完整银行卡号。
  • 激活信息：签发和支持单机许可证所需的机器申请码、其中包含的机器指纹信息、许可证编号和激活状态。
  • 支持信息：用户寻求帮助时自愿发送的消息、诊断信息和文件。
  • 网站访问数据：GitHub Pages 为交付和保护本网站，可能处理 IP 地址、浏览器类型和请求时间等普通服务器日志。

3. 信息用途

信息仅用于处理和记录订单、签发与验证许可证、提供支持、防止滥用、处理争议、维护安全，以及履行法律、税务和会计义务。

4. 本地应用数据

Dinorun 将设置、许可证和存档数据保存在用户的 Windows 电脑本地，不会自动上传。只有用户主动为获得支持而发送时，我们才会收到这些文件。

5. 服务提供商

为运营产品，必要信息可能由相关服务商处理，包括结账页面显示的支付服务商、托管网站的 GitHub Pages，以及用于客户支持的电子邮件服务商。使用这些服务时，其各自的隐私条款也适用。

6. 保存期限

因许可管理、反欺诈、税务、会计、审计或争议处理需要，订单、许可证和必要支持记录可能保存最长七年。其他支持材料将在不再有合理需要时删除。

7. 安全与跨境处理

我们采用合理的技术和组织措施保护记录。服务提供商可能在用户所在国家或地区以外处理信息。任何互联网传输或存储系统都无法保证绝对安全。

8. 用户选择与权利

在完成身份核验，并遵守法律强制保存以及正当许可和交易记录要求的前提下，用户可以申请访问、更正或删除个人信息。请通过上述客服邮箱联系。

9. 儿童

Dinorun 不面向儿童，我们不会故意收集儿童个人信息。

10. 政策变更

产品、服务提供商或法律要求变化时，我们可能更新本政策，并同步更新页面上的生效日期。
